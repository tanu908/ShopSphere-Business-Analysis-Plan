# ShopSphere-Business-Analysis-Plan
This Business Analysis Plan (v1.0) serves as the strategic framework and foundational roadmap for ShopSphere’s Q4 2026 Customer Experience and Seller Growth Enhancement Project. Designed to bridge business objectives with technical execution, the document outlines the end-to-end business analysis lifecycle required to modernize ShopSphere’s Progressive Web App (PWA) marketplace.   The primary objective of this project is to drive core growth metrics—specifically achieving a 25% relative improvement in customer retention (increasing from a baseline of 18% to 22.5%) and a 40% increase in monthly seller onboarding (expanding from 50 to 70 sellers per month). To deliver these outcomes within an 8-week timeline and a budget of ₹15 Lakhs, the plan structures requirements across three high-impact digital initiatives:   Personalized Recommendation Engine: Enhancing product discovery using customer activity and purchase history while ensuring compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.   Express / Saved-Payment Checkout: Streamlining cart conversion and lowering abandonment rates below 50% via 1-tap UPI Intent flows and tokenized card checkout adhering to RBI 2FA regulations.   Seller Analytics Dashboard: Empowering marketplace vendors with actionable visibility into sales performance, inventory levels, and return metrics.   Aligned with international IIBA / BABOK (Business Analysis Body of Knowledge) standards, this document establishes clear scope boundaries, stakeholder engagement schedules, and a structured 5-phase timeline covering Planning, Elicitation, Requirements Management, Solution Design, and UAT Evaluation. Furthermore, it details essential governance mechanisms—including a Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM), MoSCoW prioritization, RACI matrix, and risk mitigation strategies—to ensure 100% artifact delivery and quality assurance before development begins
